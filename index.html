    FAQ

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about renting a car with LCR in Mutare.

    Still have questions?

    Our team is available 24/7 to answer any questions about our services, pricing, or availability.

    What documents do I need to rent a car?

    You'll need a valid government-issued ID, a valid Zimbabwe driving licence (at least 1 year old), and a credit or debit card for the security deposit. International renters need an International Driving Permit (IDP).

    Is insurance included in the rental price?

    Yes! All LCR rentals include basic third-party insurance at no extra cost. We also offer comprehensive collision damage waivers (CDW) as optional add-ons during checkout.

    Can I cancel or modify my booking?

    Free cancellation is available up to 24 hours before your scheduled pickup. Modifications can be made up to 12 hours before pickup, subject to availability.

    What is the minimum age to rent a car?

    The minimum age to rent a standard vehicle is 21 years. For luxury vehicles (Mercedes, BMW, Tesla), the minimum age is 25 with at least 3 years of licensed driving experience.

    Are there mileage limits on rentals?

    Standard rentals come with 200 km/day free mileage. Unlimited mileage plans are available as an upgrade for longer trips or outstation travel.

    Can I take the car outside Mutare?

    Yes! Outstation travel within Zimbabwe is permitted. Travel to neighbouring countries requires special permits — contact us for details.

    What happens if the car breaks down?

    Our 24/7 roadside assistance team will be dispatched immediately. If the vehicle cannot be repaired quickly, we'll arrange a replacement car at no additional cost.

    How does the security deposit work?

    A refundable security deposit is held on your card at pickup and fully released within 3–5 business days after the vehicle is returned in its original condition.
