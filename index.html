    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about renting with LCR in Mutare. Can't find an answer? Reach out to our team anytime.

    How does online booking and approval work?

    Booking with LCR is simple and fast. Submit your reservation online in under 2 minutes. You'll instantly receive a unique booking reference number.

    1
    Submit

    Fill in the booking form and confirm your details.

    2
    Review

    Our team reviews your request within 15–30 minutes.

    3
    Confirm

    You receive a confirmation and pickup instructions.

    4
    Drive

    Collect your vehicle and hit the road!

    Use the Track Booking button at any time to check your reservation status.
    Can I cancel or modify my booking?

    We offer flexible cancellation and modification options:

    Free CancellationUp to 24 hours before pickup — no charge
    Late CancellationWithin 24 hours — 20% of booking value
    ModificationsUp to 12 hours before pickup, subject to availability
    No-ShowFull booking fee applies
    Cancel or track your booking instantly using your reference number via the Track Booking portal.
    How far in advance should I book?

    We accept same-day bookings, but we recommend booking at least 24–48 hours in advance to guarantee your preferred vehicle. During peak seasons (school holidays, public holidays, December), book 2–3 weeks ahead to avoid disappointment.

    Booking further in advance also unlocks better availability across our full fleet.

    What is the minimum age to rent a car?
    Standard VehiclesMinimum age: 21 years with 1+ year licence
    Luxury VehiclesMinimum age: 25 years with 3+ years experience (Mercedes, BMW, Tesla)

    How does the security deposit work?

    A refundable security deposit is pre-authorised on your card at vehicle pickup. No money is charged unless there is damage, excess mileage, or fuel shortfall.

    Economy / SedanDeposit: $150 USD
    SUV / 4x4Deposit: $250 USD
    Release Time3–5 business days after return

    Within Zimbabwe

    Allowed
    South Africa / Mozambique / Zambia
